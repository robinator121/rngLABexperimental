What to measure:

Termikus zaj (Johnson–Nyquist-zaj)	Thermal noise	Measure electrical noise across a resistor using an amplifier and fast ADC
Dióda lavinazaja	Avalanche diode noise	Measure random current fluctuations in a reverse-biased diode or transistor
Fotodióda sörétzaja	Photodiode shot noise	Measure fluctuations caused by discrete photon arrivals
Képérzékelő sötétzaja	CMOS sensor dark noise	Record RAW camera frames in darkness and analyze pixel noise
Rádiófrekvenciás háttérzaj	Radio-frequency background noise	Capture raw RF samples using an SDR receiver
Légköri részecskék érkezési időközei	Atmospheric particle arrival intervals	Timestamp individual particle-detection pulses from an optical particle counter
Szenzorok elektromos mérési zaja	Sensor electronic measurement noise	Record raw analog readings from light, temperature or other sensors
Órajel-fáziszaj és időzítési jitter	Clock phase noise and timing jitter	Measure timing fluctuations between independent oscillators
